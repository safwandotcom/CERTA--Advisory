A place worth the move.

Malaysia pairs internationally ranked universities with one of Southeast Asia's most liveable, multicultural capitals: English-medium teaching, a cost of living well below the UK, Australia or Canada, and Kuala Lumpur's international airport putting you a short flight from home.

  • Cost of livingWell below the UK, Australia and Canada
  • LanguageMost degrees taught in English
  • ClimateTropical, ~27°C year-round average
  • PeopleMalay, Chinese, Indian and international communities

EMGS Registered

Your visa, handled by people who do this daily.

EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services) is the Malaysian government body responsible for processing international student visas and passes. We handle your EMGS visa and pass processing on your behalf, so the paperwork isn't something you have to navigate alone.

Malaysian Public Universities

Universiti Malaya (UM)

Kuala Lumpur

Malaysia's oldest university, established in 1962 from the merger of King Edward VII College of Medicine and Raffles College. Consistently ranked the country's top research university.

Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM)

Bangi, Selangor

Established in 1970 as Malaysia's national university. Grown from 3 faculties to 13, with a strong research-institute network.

Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM)

Seri Kembangan, Selangor

Founded in 1931 as the School of Agriculture, renamed UPM in 1997 as its scope broadened. Known for agriculture, forestry, veterinary medicine and research.

Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM)

George Town, Penang

Founded in 1969, Malaysia's second university. Selected in 2008 as the country's first APEX (Accelerated Programme for Excellence) university.

Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM)

Skudai, Johor

Traces back to 1904, formally established as UTM in 1975. Ranked among the world's top 100 for engineering and technology.

Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM)

Shah Alam, Selangor

Founded in 1956 to support rural Malay development. Now Malaysia's largest public university system, spanning 13 state campuses.

International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM)

Gombak, Selangor

Established in 1983 with international co-sponsorship from Malaysia and eight other countries via the OIC. Integrates Islamic values across disciplines, taught in English and Arabic.

Universiti Utara Malaysia (UUM)

Sintok, Kedah

Established in 1984 as Malaysia's sixth public university. The country's only public university focused solely on management, business, economics, law and IT.

Malaysian Private Universities

Taylor's University

Subang Jaya, Selangor

Founded in 1969, gained full university status in 2010. Hospitality and tourism ranks #17 globally in the QS World University Rankings by subject.

UCSI University

Kuala Lumpur

Founded in 1986 as a computer training institute, elevated to full university status in 2008. Offers more than 150 undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

University of Cyberjaya

Cyberjaya, Selangor

Established in 2005 as a medical sciences college, granted full university status in 2019. Known for healthcare programmes: medicine, nursing and pharmaceutical sciences.

Sunway University

Petaling Jaya, Selangor

Founded in 1987, gained full university status in 2011. Industry-relevant programmes in business, science and technology, with a strong sustainability focus.

Asia Pacific University (APU)

Kuala Lumpur

Founded in 1993 as a computing institute, became a university in 2021. Malaysia's leading tech-focused university, strong in computing, engineering and business.

INTI International University

Putra Nilai, Negeri Sembilan

Founded in 1986. Runs Malaysia's largest American degree-transfer network, with credit agreements at over 300 universities in the US and Canada.

SEGi University

Kota Damansara, Selangor

Founded in 1977, gained university status in 2012. Broad programme range across business, technology, creative arts and dentistry.

Multimedia University (MMU)

Cyberjaya & Melaka

Founded in 1994 by Telekom Malaysia, became the country's first private university in 1996. Known for digital technology, engineering and creative multimedia.

Management and Science University (MSU)

Shah Alam, Selangor

Founded in 2001, gained full university status in 2004. Broad programmes across management, health sciences, engineering and IT.

Universiti Tenaga Nasional (UNITEN)

Kajang, Selangor

Began in 1976 as Tenaga Nasional Berhad's (TNB) training institute, became a university in 1997. Combines academic study with hands-on engineering and energy-industry training.

HELP University

Damansara Heights, Kuala Lumpur

Founded in 1986, gained university college status in 2004. Home to one of Malaysia's largest psychology programmes, plus business and social science degrees.

MAHSA University

Bandar Saujana Putra, Selangor

Founded in 2004, gained full university status in 2009. Known for medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and nursing.

International Branch Campuses

Monash University Malaysia

Bandar Sunway, Selangor

Established in 1998 as the first foreign university campus in Malaysia. Comprehensive offerings across engineering, medicine and business, drawing students from more than 80 countries.

University of Nottingham Malaysia

Semenyih, Selangor

Established in 2000. Holds a five-star Malaysia Research Assessment rating, the only foreign university to do so consecutively, with particular strength in biotechnology and AI research.

Heriot-Watt University Malaysia

Putrajaya

Opened in 2014, Malaysia's first purpose-built "Green Campus." Degrees identical to Heriot-Watt's UK campuses, strong in engineering and business.

Curtin University Malaysia

Miri, Sarawak

Established in 1999, Curtin's largest campus outside Australia. Programmes span commerce, engineering, computational sciences and health sciences.

Xiamen University Malaysia

Sepang, Selangor

Opened in 2016, the first overseas campus of a Chinese public university. Parent institution Xiamen University (China) was founded in 1921.

University of Wollongong (UOW) Malaysia

Glenmarie, Selangor & George Town, Penang

Founded in 1983 as KDU College, renamed UOW Malaysia in 2019 as part of the University of Wollongong Australia global network. Main campus moved to Utropolis Glenmarie in 2015, with a second campus in Penang.

Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak Campus

Kuching, Sarawak

Established in 2000, gained university status in 2004. Jointly owned by Swinburne University of Technology Australia and the Sarawak State Government, offering degrees identical to the Australian campus.
